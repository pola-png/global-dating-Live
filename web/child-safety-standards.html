Absolute Zero-Tolerance Policy

Global Dating Chat, developed by VineBre Apps, maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy regarding any content or behavior that harms or exploits children. We explicitly prohibit all forms of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

Our Standards and Guidelines

To ensure a safe environment for our community and to prevent child abuse, the following guidelines are strictly enforced:

  • No CSAE/CSAM: Users are strictly prohibited from uploading, sharing, requesting, or distributing any content that displays, describes, promotes, or facilitates Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).
  • Minimum Age Requirement: Global Dating Chat is strictly for adults aged 18 and older. Minors are not allowed to register or use the service under any circumstances.
  • No Solicitation or Grooming: Any attempts to groom, solicit, contact, or sexually exploit minors will result in immediate and permanent account ban and referral to appropriate law enforcement authorities.

Reporting and Enforcement

We actively monitor and moderate our platform to identify and eliminate violations of our Child Safety Standards:

  • Immediate Account Termination: Any user account found violating these standards will be permanently terminated without notice.
  • Law Enforcement Collaboration: We report all instances of CSAE and CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and comply fully with local and international law enforcement agencies.
  • In-App Reporting: Users can instantly block and report any profile, post, photo, or message directly within the app's interface.
