Explicit CSAE Zero-Tolerance Policy

Dating Connect, developed by Digiskills Consult, maintains an absolute, zero-tolerance policy against any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). We are fully committed to providing a safe digital ecosystem and protecting children from harm.

Explicit Prohibitions and Proactive Enforcement

In full compliance with Google Play Child Safety Standards policies, Digiskills Consult explicitly prohibits:

  • Strict Prohibition of CSAE and CSAM: The production, generation, uploading, sharing, requesting, storing, or transmission of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) is strictly forbidden across all app features, including private messages, group chats, live streams, and profile images.
  • Prohibition of Grooming and Exploitation: Any behavior attempting to groom, coerce, solicit, harass, or sexually exploit a minor is prohibited and results in immediate permanent expulsion.
  • Strict Adult-Only Service (18+ Requirement): Dating Connect is exclusively designed for adults aged 18 and older. Minors are barred from registering or accessing the app under any circumstances.

Reporting, Detection, and Law Enforcement Cooperation

We actively protect our user community through continuous oversight and rapid resolution mechanisms:

  • Automated & Human Moderation: Content posted on Dating Connect is screened to detect and prevent unauthorized media or prohibited interactions.
  • NCMEC Reporting: Any detected or reported instance of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or CSAE is immediately reported directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and relevant international law enforcement agencies.
  • In-App Reporting Tools: Users can flag any user profile, message, photo, or live activity directly inside the app for immediate review by our moderation team.
