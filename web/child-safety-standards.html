Dating Connect Safety Standards Against CSAE

Application Name: Dating Connect

Developer Name: Digiskills Consult

Package ID: datingconnect.app

Effective Date: September 2026

This document sets out the official, public Child Safety Standards for Dating Connect, developed and operated by Digiskills Consult. It details the rules strictly prohibiting Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), user obligations, reporting procedures, automated moderation systems, and legal enforcement consequences.

1. Purpose of these Safety Standards

These Safety Standards articulate Digiskills Consult's absolute prohibition against child sexual abuse and exploitation. They apply across all features of Dating Connect, including user profiles, private chats, group messaging, live streaming, video calls, photo uploads, and customer support. These standards ensure rapid enforcement and make clear that Dating Connect prohibits any use of the service that sexualizes, endangers, exploits, grooms, or harms minors.

2. Zero-Tolerance Standard Against CSAE & CSAM

Digiskills Consult maintains a zero-tolerance policy for Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). Users must not use Dating Connect to create, upload, post, store, request, advertise, promote, search for, share, sell, distribute, exchange, or facilitate CSAM/CSAE content, grooming, sextortion, child trafficking, or minor sexualization. This rule applies whether the conduct is explicit, coded, disguised, attempted, threatened, encouraged, or coordinated off-platform.

3. Content That Is Strictly Prohibited

Users are strictly prohibited from posting, requesting, or sharing:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in any form.
  • Nudity, sexualized imagery, or explicit depictions involving minors (individuals under 18 years of age).
  • Manipulated, synthetic, or AI-generated sexualized imagery depicting minors.
  • Exploitative role-play, stories, or descriptions involving minors.
  • Content that normalizes, praises, glorifies, or jokes about child abuse or sexual contact with minors.
  • Directories, links, hashtags, or coded tags intended to locate or distribute exploitative material.

4. Strict Adult-Only Service (18+ Requirement)

Dating Connect is exclusively designed and operated for adults aged 18 years and older. Minors are strictly prohibited from registering an account, accessing the app, or participating in any community features. Age verification screens and automated checks are enforced during onboarding.

5. Grooming, Solicitation, and Predatory Conduct

Users must not use Dating Connect to contact minors for sexual purposes, build trust with a minor for sexual exploitation, solicit private photos or videos from minors, request personal contact details from minors, coerce minors into secrecy, or pressure minors into private channels. Any attempt to groom, blackmail, or extort minors results in an immediate permanent ban and law enforcement referral.

6. Off-Platform Coordination Prohibited

Users must not use Dating Connect to direct any user, and especially any minor, to external encrypted messaging platforms, hidden websites, or off-platform links for sexual exploitation, trafficking, or moderation evasion. Evasion attempts remain severe violations regardless of where the ultimate action takes place.

7. Evasion, Disguise, and Coded Behavior

Users must not use coded terminology, symbols, hashtags, shortened URLs, indirect requests, fake age claims, blurred previews, or staged profiles to conceal child sexual abuse or exploitation activity. Impersonating a minor or falsifying age details to gain access to children is grounds for immediate expulsion.

8. User Reporting Obligations and In-App Tools

Users who encounter any suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation content on Dating Connect must report it immediately:

  • In-App Reporting: Use the dedicated report button on any profile, message, photo, group chat, or live stream to instantly flag content for moderation review.
  • Urgent Email Reporting: Submit direct reports to our safety team at ubongp.udoka@gmail.com.

Users must not reshare, screenshot, download, or circulate suspected abusive material.

9. Moderation, Evidence Preservation & NCMEC Reporting

Digiskills Consult actively safeguards the platform through automated filtering, media scanning, and human moderation. When suspected CSAM/CSAE material is identified:

  • Content is immediately removed and account access is terminated.
  • Relevant evidence, account details, and logs are preserved as required by law.
  • Incidents are reported directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and relevant global law enforcement authorities.

10. Account & Access Consequences

Accounts involved in child sexual abuse or exploitation are permanently banned, blocked from re-registration, and denied access to all Digiskills Consult services. Associated device identifiers, IP addresses, and linked accounts are permanently blacklisted.

11. Child Safety Priority

Child safety violations are non-negotiable. Concerns regarding CSAE override user status, account tenure, monetization, or popularity. Zero tolerance is enforced universally without exception.
