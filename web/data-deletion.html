Data Deletion Policy for Global Dating Chat

Account Deletion Process

Users can request complete deletion of their account and associated data through the following methods:

Method 1: In-App Deletion

  1. Open the Global Dating Chat app
  2. Go to Profile, then scroll to the account section
  3. Select "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm the deletion request

Method 2: Email Request

Send an email to globaldatingchat@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
  • Your registered email address
  • Reason for deletion, if you want to share it

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, we remove your account and associated user data from our systems, including:

  • Your profile information, photos, and avatar image
  • Your messages in private chats and group chats
  • Your posts and photo posts
  • Your live stream and live message history
  • Your video call records and support session records
  • Your reports and moderation records that you created

Data Deletion Timeline

  • Immediate: Account access is removed
  • Within 7 days: Remaining user-visible content is removed or detached where possible
  • Within 30 days: Complete purge from primary systems is attempted
  • Backup systems: May retain data for a limited period before automatic purge

Data That May Be Retained

For legal, fraud prevention, or safety reasons, we may retain:

  • Abuse or moderation records where required by law or safety policy
  • Aggregated analytics data that cannot identify you personally
  • Transaction or billing records if applicable and required by law

Contact for Deletion & Safety Reports

Email: globaldatingchat@gmail.com
Response Time: Within 48 hours

Read our Child Safety Standards.

This policy is intended to support Google Play account-deletion requirements and user data transparency.
