3. Child Safety Standards & CSAE Zero-Tolerance

Dating Connect (developed by Digiskills Consult) enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

  • Creation, uploading, transmission, or solicitation of CSAE/CSAM is prohibited.
  • Violations result in immediate permanent expulsion, evidence preservation, and direct reporting to NCMEC CyberTipline and law enforcement authorities.
  • Read our complete, published Child Safety Standards Page for complete details.

4. Data Storage, Rights & Deletion

You can request complete deletion of your account and data at any time inside the app or by visiting our Data Deletion Page.
