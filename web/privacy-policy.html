Child Safety Standards and CSAE Policy

Dating Connect (developer name: Digiskills Consult) has a zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), and any form of child harm or endangerment.

  • We strictly prohibit the creation, upload, sharing, transmission, or solicitation of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) content or Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • We strictly prohibit grooming, sexual harassment, or exploitation of minors.
  • Any user found producing, requesting, or sharing CSAE content or attempting to engage with minors will be immediately and permanently banned from Dating Connect, and reported directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement authorities.
  • We maintain in-app user reporting mechanisms and content moderation tools to promptly detect, remove, and report any violation of child safety standards.
